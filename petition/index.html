How this is counted

Counts come from the petition's signature export. No names, street addresses or full postal codes are published here: only counts by postal area, town, region and day. Signers were placed on the map by the first part of their postal code using GeoNames centroids, or by the town they typed if there was no usable code. Map labels show a town name only when at least five signers typed it, otherwise the postal area's official name. "Vancouver Island & Gulf Islands" means Canadian postal codes starting V8, V9 or V0R. Map outlines: Natural Earth.

Electoral and regional districts: each Canadian signer is placed at the centre of their postal area. In rural postal areas (those with 0 as the second character), which can cross district lines, the town they typed is used instead where it can be matched. A few signers near a boundary may therefore be counted in the neighbouring district. Districts with 1 to 4 signers show "fewer than 5". Boundaries: Elections BC (2023 redistribution), Elections Canada (2023 Representation Order) and the B.C. Administrative Boundaries Management System.
