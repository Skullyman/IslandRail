How this is counted

The planner sends a small, anonymous note when it opens and when someone uses a feature, at most once per feature a visit. There are no cookies and nothing is stored in your browser. Your address is never saved: visitors are counted with a one-way scrambled code that changes every day and is deleted after two days, so visits can't be linked from one day to the next. Nothing is sent if your browser asks sites not to track you (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control). Saved views are counted from the share links themselves, including those made before these counts began.
